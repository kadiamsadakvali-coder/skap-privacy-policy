<h2>3. Information We Collect and Process</h2>

<p>Depending on how you use the application, the following information may be processed:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Engineer profile information that you enter.</li>
  <li>Machine and equipment names and technical details.</li>
  <li>Alignment measurements and calculation results.</li>
  <li>Vibration readings and analysis results.</li>
  <li>Job records, reports, and related engineering information.</li>
  <li>AI prompts, messages, and technical context submitted when using AI-assisted features.</li>
  <li>Technical information required to operate and protect the AI service, such as temporary client IP information used for rate limiting.</li>
</ul>

<p>We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information for the normal operation of the alignment and vibration analysis features.</p>

<h2>4. Local Data Storage</h2>

<p>SK Align Pro currently stores application information, including profile details, jobs, measurements, and reports, locally on your device using browser-based local storage.</p>

<p>As a result:</p>

<ul>
  <li>This information is generally stored on your device rather than in a permanent SK Align Pro user database.</li>
  <li>Clearing application data, browser storage, or uninstalling the application may permanently remove locally stored information.</li>
  <li>We do not currently provide a permanent cloud database or automatic cross-device synchronization for these local records.</li>
</ul>

<p>You are responsible for maintaining appropriate exports or backups of important engineering records.</p>

<h2>5. AI-Assisted Features and Google Gemini</h2>

<p>SK Align Pro provides AI-assisted functionality through a backend service.</p>

<p>When you use AI features:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Your submitted prompts, conversation content, and relevant technical context may be sent to the SK Align Pro backend over HTTPS.</li>
  <li>The backend forwards the information required to provide the AI response to Google Gemini.</li>
  <li>Relevant technical context may include machine names and engineering measurements.</li>
  <li>Your local engineer profile's personal information is not intentionally included in these AI requests.</li>
</ul>

<p>AI-generated results may contain errors or incomplete technical interpretations. Users must independently verify engineering calculations, recommendations, and safety-critical decisions before acting on them.</p>

<p>Do not submit confidential, personal, or commercially sensitive information unless you are authorized to do so.</p>

<p>Google's processing and retention of information are also subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.</p>

<h2>6. Server Security and Technical Logs</h2>

<p>The AI backend uses temporary in-memory IP-based rate limiting to help prevent excessive requests. The rate-limit information is maintained temporarily in server memory, with an approximate 60-second rate-limit window.</p>

<p>Standard hosting infrastructure and error logs may also be generated as part of operating and maintaining the service.</p>

<p>We do not claim that third-party hosting or AI service providers retain no technical information. Their applicable policies and operational practices govern information processed by those services.</p>

<h2>7. Third-Party Services</h2>

<p>SK Align Pro may use the following third-party services:</p>

<p><strong>Google Gemini:</strong> Used to provide AI-assisted responses.</p>

<p><strong>Google Fonts:</strong> Fonts may be loaded from Google's font delivery infrastructure when required by the application.</p>

<p><strong>Hosting and infrastructure providers:</strong> Used to host and operate the application backend and website.</p>

<p>These providers may process technical information necessary to provide their services. Their own privacy policies and terms apply to their processing.</p>

<p>We do not currently use advertising networks or analytics SDKs in the application, based on the reviewed application implementation.</p>

<h2>8. Android Device Storage and Backup</h2>

<p>SK Align Pro may store application information locally on your Android device.</p>

<p>Android device backup behavior may depend on the application's backup configuration, Android version, device settings, and the backup services enabled by the user.</p>

<p>Users should understand that locally stored information may be affected by device backup, restoration, uninstall, or application-data clearing operations.</p>

<h2>9. Data Retention and Deletion</h2>

<p>Locally stored information remains on your device until you delete it, clear the application data, uninstall the application, or otherwise remove the relevant stored information.</p>

<p>AI prompts and technical information sent to third-party services may be processed or retained according to the applicable service providers' policies.</p>
